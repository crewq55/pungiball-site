PUNGiBALL — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how the arcade game PUNGiBALL ("the App") handles your information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data (optional): if you register, we store your email address and a hashed password managed by Firebase Authentication. If you play as a guest, only an anonymous user ID is created. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and email.
  • Game progress: level, experience, coins, owned characters, upgrades, rank points, selected avatar and language preference. This is stored in Firebase Realtime Database to sync your progress.
  • Support messages: if you contact support from the in-game chat, we store the message and the email you provide so we can answer you.
  • Local data: settings (language, volumes, graphics quality, notification and vibration preferences) are stored on your device.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or device files.
  • No analytics or advertising tracking SDKs are currently included.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except the providers below.

3. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database) hosts account and progress data. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google, only if you choose "Sign in with Google".
  • Google Translate (unofficial endpoint) may receive the text of in-game announcements to translate them into your language. No personal data is sent with them.

4. Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the App. This permanently removes your Firebase account and your cloud save. Local data is removed when you uninstall the App.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Guest play requires no personal information.

6. Security

Communication with Firebase uses HTTPS. Passwords are never stored by us: they are handled only by Firebase Authentication.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us through the in-game support bot or at the contact email published on the Play Store listing.
